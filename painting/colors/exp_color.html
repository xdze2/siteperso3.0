Color Matcher (AZERTY Controls)

Inspired by Josef Albers' Interaction of Color (1963) and his Homage to the Square series. The same color looks different depending on what surrounds it — try toggling the outer backgrounds to see the inner square shift.

See this video for an introduction.

The game: the left square shows a random target color. Use the HSL sliders (or AZERTY keys) to match it on the right. Each square sits on a different background, which biases your perception — that's the whole point. Hit Check Accuracy to score your guess.
